# Replaceable demonstration media

These files are bundled locally. No third-party image/video hotlinks or runtime font requests are required.

- grand-tourer.jpg: original AI-generated fictional coupe concept image created for this demo.
- adventure.jpg: original AI-generated fictional SUV concept image created for this demo.
- driving-study.mp4: original 10-second, silent H.264 motion study rendered from the coupe image, with a subtle looping virtual camera move. This is an animated concept image, not driving footage.

The concept visuals are illustrative, not photographs of the inventory makes or models. Do not present them as actual stock photos. Replace with your own dealership vehicle photography and properly licensed automotive footage before a real launch.

Set the hero image and MP4 paths/URLs in Customize dealership. Empty the MP4 field to use a still image. The default MP4 is 1280×720, 24 fps, ~1.2 MB, H.264/yuv420p, silent, and fast-start optimized. Use an equivalent muted, short, looping MP4. Keep the fallback poster high quality and under ~500 KB. The included JPEGs are ~340 KB each at 1672×941.

Reduced-motion and Save-Data preferences suppress the video request. Video pauses out of view, in background tabs, or through the pause control. An error leaves the poster visible. Autoplay restrictions leave an explicit play control.
